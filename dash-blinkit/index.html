Why P95, not the average

A store can show a healthy ~40s average while its P95 sits near the 60s wall — the slowest 5% of orders breach SLA every hour. At scale that's hundreds of late deliveries a day. P95 protects the 10-minute promise.

Why DOI < 1.5 days is the trigger

Replenishment from hub to dark store takes ~6–8 hours. Below 1.5 days of inventory you have less than one replenishment cycle of cover left — the stockout danger zone.
