The one-line thesis

Eloelo scaled live social (games, audio) and then StoryTV micro-drama into one of India’s biggest attention machines — 33M MAU, 95 min/day — now backed by NBCUniversal. The content and engagement are real; the 4.86★ rating is not the metric to trust. The analyst job: engagement → gifting monetisation, and a real health metric instead of a prompted one.

01 · The product

Attention, live and on-demand

Join a live game or audio room, or binge StoryTV micro-dramas. The loop mixes live interaction (gifting) with on-demand bingeing (coins) — two monetisation engines in one group.

Who

Young, mobile-first India — entertainment + social, often Tier-2/3.

How it earns

Gifting and coins in live rooms, plus coin unlocks on StoryTV.

Why it wins

95 min/day is category-leading engagement; NBCUniversal brings content and global reach.

02 · Business & unit economics

Monetised by a whale cohort

Live social earns on gifting/coins concentrated in a small set of heavy payers. Contribution rides payer depth against creator payouts and content cost.

Payer depth > payer count

A few whales drive gifting. The lever is deepening and retaining them, not just adding users.

Engagement feeds revenue

StoryTV’s 95 min/day is the attention that converts to coin spend — protect it from post-update breakage.

Analyst read

The north star is engaged users × payer depth — measured on real engagement, not a star rating that looks prompted. A health metric you can trust is step one.

07 · Moat & risk

What protects it, what threatens it

The moat

  • Engagement — 95 min/day on StoryTV.
  • NBCUniversal — content + global reach.
  • Multi-format — live + on-demand.

The risks

  • Measurement — an inflated rating hides churn.
  • Whale dependence — revenue concentrated in few payers.
  • Trust & safety at live-social scale.
